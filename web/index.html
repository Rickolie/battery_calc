Your grid connection

The size of your main fuse, shown on the meter cabinet or your grid operator's bill: 1 × = single-phase, 3 × = three-phase, the number is amps per phase (most Dutch homes: 3 × 25 A). It sets the maximum battery power (230 V × amps per phase). On a single-phase connection, three-phase batteries and kilns above 3.68 kW are left out.

Safety room kept free on each phase for the rest of the house (oven, heat pump, car). 20% means a battery may use at most 80% of a phase: 4.6 kW on 25 A. A higher margin lowers the battery power used in the simulation.

Prices and future rules

Which price the payback is calculated with. NL current: today's lowest price in Dutch shops, incl. VAT. NL lowest-ever: the lowest price the scraper has seen. DE 0% VAT: German manufacturer shops without VAT (only delivered in Germany; check whether you can buy there). Black Friday: the real deal found during the Black Friday window, or (est.) an estimate with the discount set below. "All variants" shows them side by side.

From 2027 to 2029 suppliers must pay at least 50% of the energy price for power you export. From 2030 that legal minimum ends. 0% assumes you then only get what the supplier offers; 30% is a milder scenario. A lower value makes storing your own solar power more valuable.

Dynamic contracts and battery trading are tested against real day-ahead prices of past years. Last 3 years (2023–2025) is the headline and best reflects today's market; the full history adds the calm pre-2021 years and the 2022 crisis as a range. Full history takes about three times longer.

Batteries

The shortlist holds 10 representative plug-in batteries (about 5 minutes). All batteries compares every size and brand in the dataset (34 configurations) and can take 15–30 minutes in the browser.

One battery is worked out in more detail: the yearly earnings per strategy (where the money comes from) and the pottery kiln objective. "Automatic" picks the battery with the fastest payback.

Black Friday

During the Black Friday window (20 November – 1 December) the price scraper checks the shops every 4 hours and records the lowest price it finds as the real deal, next to the last normal price. The report then shows a "Black Friday quick decision" table with the deal, its discount and the payback. Until real deals are found, the estimate below is used.

Pottery kiln on free power

How many days per year a kiln can do a full firing on solar surplus (plus the chosen battery), with at most 5% of its energy from the grid. Take the values from your kiln's data sheet.

Length of one firing to the highest temperature you use (stoneware about 1250–1300 °C), including the slow start. Typical: 7–10 hours.

%

A kiln switches its elements on and off; on average it draws only part of its rated power. Energy per firing = rated kW × hours × this share (8 h × 70% = 5.6 kWh per rated kW). Use 100% to be on the safe side.
